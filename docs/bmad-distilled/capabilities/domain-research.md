## Discovery (init — do NOT research yet)

Write an **initial scope document immediately** for review; confirm before proceeding.

## The research dimensions (work in order, web-verify each)
1. **Domain analysis** — structure, value chain, key players, market dynamics, maturity.
2. **Competitive landscape** — dominant firms & strategies, emerging entrants, differentiation, positioning gaps.
3. **Regulatory focus** — governing bodies, standards, compliance requirements, pending regulation and its impact.
4. **Technical trends** — emerging technologies, adoption curves, innovation shaping the domain.

For each: show your web-search analysis *before* findings; cite each claim with a source URL; flag confidence on soft data.

## Finalize — the synthesis document
One authoritative document with full structure:
- **Executive summary** + table of contents.
- **Introduction & methodology** (significance now, scope, sources, framework, goals achieved).
- **Domain analysis & dynamics** (structure, value chain, key players, maturity).
- **Competitive landscape & positioning.**
- **Regulatory & compliance landscape.**
- **Technical trends & innovation.**
- **Strategic synthesis & recommendations** (opportunities, risks & mitigation, implementation guidance, future outlook).
- **Methodology & source documentation** (queries, verification, confidence, limitations) + appendices.
